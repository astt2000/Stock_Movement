1 Price Threshold Parameters (SUP, RES, TGT)

These values establish predictive psychological parameters mapped from immediate price distribution channels:

• SUP (Support Level - 95% anchor): The price baseline "floor". Buyer accumulation typically increases here to protect downside breakdown.

• RES (Resistance Level - 104% anchor): The price channel "ceiling". Technical selling pressure increases as short-term traders liquidate gains.

• TGT (Target Level - 110% anchor): The medium-term momentum pattern target objective upon a clean resistance breakout.

EXAMPLE: If Nvidia (NVDA) ticks at $100.00:
SUP = $95.00 (Stop-loss floor) | RES = $104.00 (Take-profit celling) | TGT = $110.00 (Breakout objective)
2 RSI-14 Index (Relative Strength Index)

A high-fidelity momentum oscillator bounding velocity structures between 0 and 100 over a 14-period envelope:

• Value ≥ 65: Asset is technically overbought. Momentum velocity may be overextended, triggering distribution blocks.

• Value ≤ 35: Asset is technically oversold. Liquidation trends are exhausted, opening channel entries for accumulation cycles.

EXAMPLE: NVDA climbs +8% instantly pushing RSI to 72. System flags an Overbought condition warning of near-term mean reversion risks.
3 MACD Structural Phase (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)

Tracks velocity shifts between short-term exponential parameters and trend baselines:

• Bullish Crossover: Triggered when positive daily variance moves above 0.5%, signaling expanding upside velocity.

• Bearish Realignment: Triggered when daily variance slides below -0.5%, signaling downside rotation parameters.

• Neutral Phase: Triggered inside the -0.5% to +0.5% consolidation boundaries, signaling range-bound tightening vectors.

EXAMPLE: If NVDA falls -2.4%, MACD switches to "Bearish Realignment", shifting the overall suggested execution signal toward "SELL".
